What we don't do

Some work needs a licensed trade by law. We'll tell you which one to call.

  • Electrical, including power points, lights and hardwired smoke alarms
  • Plumbing, drainage and gas
  • Building work over $3,300, which needs a QBCC licence
  • Asbestos. If we suspect it in an older home, we stop and tell you.
  • Air-con installation, re-gassing or repairs, which need a licensed air-conditioning technician

    Questions

    Before you book.

    Anything else, send a text. We're happy to answer.

    How much does it cost?

    The minimum charge is $220 including GST. That covers the call-out and the first hour on site. After that it's $110 an hour. Jesse gives you a fair idea of the time and price before you book, and for a bigger job Minh calls you to talk it through first. If Minh finds more on site, he tells you the price and you decide before he starts.

    Do you put together IKEA furniture?

    Yes, it's Minh's speciality, at home and in shops and offices. Text a screenshot of your IKEA order to 0485 005 568 and he'll tell you how long it'll take before you book. Book a time after your delivery arrives. Tall pieces get fixed to the wall. JP Smart is independent and isn't connected with IKEA.

    Do you clean carpets and air-conditioners?

    Yes. Carpet cleaning, and split system air-con cleaning: filters, coils and drain trays. Both are priced per room and per unit, and they fit well into the same visit as other jobs, or an end-of-lease clean-up. We only clean air-conditioners. Installing, re-gassing or repairing one needs a licensed air-conditioning technician.

    Who answers the phone?

    Jesse answers calls and texts to 0485 005 568, any time. Jesse takes the details and photos, tells you the price, books you in and sends your reminder. Minh spends his day on the tools. If you'd rather talk to Minh, Jesse can book you a phone call with him.

    How soon can you come?

    Bookings start at least 24 hours ahead, so Minh can plan the day and bring the right parts. We don't do emergency call-outs. For a burst pipe or an electrical fault, call a licensed plumber or electrician.

    Do you need a licence for this kind of work?

    In Queensland, building work of $3,300 or less (labour and materials, including GST) can be done without a QBCC licence, as long as it isn't electrical, plumbing, drainage, gas or another licensed occupation. We stay inside that. If your job goes over it or needs a licensed trade, we'll tell you before anything starts.

    Are you insured?

    Yes. JP Smart carries $10 million public liability insurance with Allianz. A copy of the certificate is available to property managers and commercial clients.

    Can you deal with my tenant directly?

    Yes. Once the owner or agency approves the job, we contact the tenant, book a time that suits them, and let you know when it's done.

    What if the job turns out bigger than it looked?

    We stop and tell you before doing anything extra. You get photos and a revised price, and nothing changes on the invoice unless you've agreed to it.
